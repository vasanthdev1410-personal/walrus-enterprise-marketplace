1. Objective
Purpose

Module 01 – Part 6 establishes the enterprise authorization architecture for the WALRUS Enterprise Marketplace Platform.

Following successful authentication, verification, and session establishment, every request for a protected resource shall undergo authorization before access is granted.

This part defines how the platform:

Evaluates permissions
Protects resources
Enforces access policies
Applies role-based access control
Records authorization decisions
Supports future authorization models

Authorization determines what an authenticated identity is permitted to do.

Authentication determines who the identity is.

These responsibilities shall remain independent throughout the platform.

Authorization Objectives

The authorization platform shall ensure:

Least privilege
Centralized policy enforcement
Resource protection
Consistent authorization decisions
Auditability
Scalability
Future extensibility

Authorization shall never replace authentication.

3. Authorization Principles

The authorization platform shall comply with the following principles.

3.1 Authentication Before Authorization

Authorization shall occur only after successful authentication.

Unauthenticated requests shall never reach authorization evaluation.

3.2 Deny by Default

Access shall be denied unless an approved authorization policy explicitly grants permission.

Implicit permission shall never exist.

3.3 Least Privilege

Every identity shall receive only the minimum permissions required to perform approved responsibilities.

Permissions shall never exceed operational requirements.

3.4 Centralized Policy

Authorization decisions shall originate from a centralized authorization service.

Business modules shall not independently implement authorization rules.

3.5 Resource Ownership

Every protected resource shall define:

Resource Owner
Allowed Roles
Required Permissions
Administrative Overrides (where approved)

Ownership shall remain explicit.

3.6 Policy Consistency

Equivalent authorization requests shall always produce equivalent decisions when evaluated under identical conditions.

Authorization logic shall remain deterministic.

3.7 Secure Failure

If authorization cannot be evaluated safely:

Access shall be denied.
Security events shall be generated.
Audit records shall be created.

Unexpected failures shall never grant access.

3.8 Auditability

Every authorization decision shall support auditing.

Events include:

Access Granted
Access Denied
Permission Evaluation
Administrative Override
Policy Failure
Resource Access Attempt

Audit logs shall never expose authentication secrets.

6. Identity Roles
Objective

Identity Roles define the functional responsibilities assigned to authenticated identities within the WALRUS Enterprise Marketplace Platform.

Roles determine the categories of permissions an identity may receive.

Roles do not authenticate an identity.

Roles do not automatically grant permissions.

Permissions are evaluated independently during authorization.

Phase 1 supports the following roles:

Admin	Approved

Finance Manager

Delivery Partner
Auditor
API Client
Service Account
Multiple Role Assignment

One identity may possess multiple roles simultaneously.

Example:

Identity

↓

Customer
Seller

Role combinations shall remain centrally managed.

Role Principles

Identity roles shall:

Be centrally assigned
Be centrally revoked
Be auditable
Support future expansion
Never bypass authorization policies
